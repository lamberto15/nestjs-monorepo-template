`GET /v1/auth/sessions` lists apps (token clients still signed in) after the browser sessions, each with `kind` (`browser` or `app`); `DELETE /v1/auth/sessions/{id}` signs either out. Devices of apps read "NestJSMobile on iOS". `PUT /v1/notifications/preferences` takes back what `GET` answers for mandatory types.
